# Analyze data

- Identify the question, source, units, time zone, time range and definition of each measure. Inspect schema, missing values, duplicates and data quality before drawing conclusions.
- Use reproducible calculations and a suitable comparison or baseline. For time-series or predictive work, preserve chronology, prevent look-ahead leakage, and separate development from evaluation data.
- Report sample size, exclusions, assumptions, uncertainty and material limitations. When assessing trading or financial data, account for relevant fees, slippage and execution assumptions; simulated results are not live returns.
- Cite or identify the source and distinguish observations from causal claims. Do not alter the source data unless requested.
